The story so far

In 2021, our founders Marcus and Priya were leading engineering teams at a mid-size SaaS company. Every week, they watched talented engineers spend hours manually exporting CSVs, copy-pasting data between tools, and building fragile one-off scripts to glue systems together.

"There has to be a better way," Marcus said. There wasn't — not one that felt like it was built for engineers and non-engineers alike. Existing automation tools were either too simple or required you to be a DevOps expert to operate.

So they left and built NexaFlow. The vision: an automation platform with the power of code and the accessibility of a visual canvas, supercharged by AI agents that could reason about complex business logic.

Three years later, NexaFlow powers over 50,000 teams globally. We've processed over 2 billion workflow executions, saved our customers an estimated 12 million engineering hours, and we're just getting started.

Our Values

What we believe in

🚀

Build Fast, Ship Often

We ship every week. Not because we're reckless — because we believe the fastest way to learn what's right is to put it in front of real people. We iterate relentlessly.

🤝

Customers First, Always

We make decisions by asking "does this make our customers more successful?" Every feature ships with documentation, migration guides, and support. We don't ship half-baked.

🌍

Radical Transparency

We publish our roadmap publicly, share our financials with the team, and post our incident reports in full detail. Trust is built through honesty, even when it's uncomfortable.

💡

Craft Over Speed

Fast doesn't mean sloppy. We care deeply about the details — the micro-interaction, the error message, the API response shape. Quality is non-negotiable.

⚖️

Responsible AI

AI agents act on behalf of real people. We take that seriously. Every agent decision is auditable, explainable, and reversible. We build guardrails, not black boxes.

🌱

Sustainability

We offset 200% of our carbon emissions and run on 100% renewable energy. Tech companies have a responsibility to the planet. We take ours seriously.
